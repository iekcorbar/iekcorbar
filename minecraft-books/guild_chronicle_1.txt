&#2b1d0eCenturies ago, this world was laid waste by a divine being known as &#8b2a1a&lShoutlander&#2b1d0e.
---
&#2b1d0eCivilizations collapsed. Much of the old world was destroyed.

And when the destruction ended, Shoutlander's creatures remained behind, among the ruins.
---
&#2b1d0eThen came several centuries of relative peace.

The ruins grew quiet. The old world lay broken, waiting.
---
&#2b1d0eAfter those long centuries, the first &#8a4f1c&lAdventurers&#2b1d0e began to arrive.

They came from other worlds.
---
&#2b1d0eFor the Adventurers are travellers between worlds. Each world is its own realm, and the Adventurers are able to move from one to the next.

It was this gift that brought them here.
