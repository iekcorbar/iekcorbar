&#8a4f1c&lThe Birthday
&#8a4f1c&lWish


&#7a5a12⚙ ━━━━━━━ ⚙


&#2b1d0e&oA tale in verse

&8by Darkmatter86
---
&#2b1d0eThe villager left his house one morning
It was a very special day
Everyone had gathered around in the town square
For it was the villager’s birthday
---
&#2b1d0eHe was greeted with cheers and confetti
Hugs and kisses with lots of gifts
The happiest day of the year, he felt it
But deep inside the forest around, the mood shifts
---
&#2b1d0ePillagers were on the prowl ready to attack
They cared not for balloons and presents
All they wanted was to cause trouble
And frighten them with their presence
---
&#2b1d0eBut to our villager, the sun beamed bright on his face
As he sat in front of a large chocolate cake
A candle lit with his friends around
As they waited to see what wish he would make
---
&#2b1d0eThey would never find out
For the wind shifted blowing out his candle
A horde of pillagers stormed the village
Way to many for the villagers and golems to handle
---
&#8b2a1a&lIt was a massacre
&#2b1d0eOne by one the villagers perished and died
The happy face of the birthday boy replaced with a sad one
As he knelt down, watched and cried
---
&#2b1d0eThen came his turn as one pillage fired an arrow into his heart
Down our boy went on his last breath
Swearing revenge on the pillagers
Who left with their loot laughing at all the death
---
&#2b1d0eThe last of the villagers alive
Buried their dead and wept heavy tears
Not to be attacked again, they left
Carrying with them what they could on top of their own fears
---
&#2b1d0eA year passed, the village was now a cobwebbed wreck
At the cemetery, a rustling below the dirt
A hand out of the ground with raging vigour
A &#8b2a1a&lrevenant&#2b1d0e in the skin of our birthday boy ready to hurt
---
&#2b1d0eThe pillagers were in celebration
Back at the outpost, they went through their treasure
Happiness and merriment abound
Filled with food and drink and all sorts of pleasure
---
&#2b1d0eThe guards on stand never saw it coming
One by one, they were ripped apart with superhuman force
The sound of screams could be heard from inside the outpost
A shuddering through the spine coursed
---
&#2b1d0eIt went quiet as one pillage looked through the window
Only to see nothing but empty dark
They gathered their weapons
Targets on their back left a mark
---
&#2b1d0eFor the &#8b2a1a&lrevenant&#2b1d0e struck through them like a knife through butter
One left standing as all others fell
Saved till last as revenge craves it
A special place in the heart to burn in the depths of hell
---
&#2b1d0eOne arrow and that was it
The revenge was short and sweet at best
A candle was lit as the &#8b2a1a&lrevenant&#2b1d0e held his killer’s head
Blowing it out, wish granted for a peaceful rest
