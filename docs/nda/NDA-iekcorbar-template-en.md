**Non-Disclosure Agreement**

between

**[OWNER LEGAL NAME]**, operating the Minecraft server and website **“iekcorbar”**
(“the Owner”),

and

**[STAFF FULL NAME]**, Discord handle **[DISCORD HANDLE]** (“the Staff Member”),

together “the Parties”, effective **[DATE]**.

**1. Confidential Information**
Confidential Information means all information about the Owner’s projects that is
not public, in any form and on any medium, including without limitation:
(a) **code and content** — source code, plugins, build scripts, resource packs,
models, textures and wiki content before publication;
(b) **roadmap and plans** — unreleased features, easter eggs, release planning,
test servers and deployment procedures;
(c) **infrastructure and business** — server configurations, VPS and hardware
details, credentials, plugin choices, costs, contracts and log/StaffHub contents;
(d) **player data** — any information about players obtained through the staff
role: identities, private messages, IP addresses, alt-accounts, reports,
punishments and statistics.

**2. Exclusions**
Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes
public through no fault of the Staff Member, (b) was demonstrably known before
the staff relationship, (c) was obtained independently without breach of this
Agreement, or (d) must be disclosed by law, provided that where permitted the
Staff Member first informs the Owner and limits the disclosure to the minimum
required.

**3. Obligations**
The Staff Member shall: (a) keep the Confidential Information secret from anyone
not authorised to know it for the server work, and not publish or share it
outside the designated channels — this explicitly includes pastebins, gists,
public or private repositories, other Discord servers, forums and social media;
(b) use the Confidential Information solely for performing staff duties for
iekcorbar; (c) keep credentials and configurations secure and never share login
details; (d) process player data only within the tools and channels provided by
the Owner; (e) report any loss, leak or suspected compromise to the Owner
without undue delay. Access granted to the Owner’s GitHub (read-only) is
personal: no inviting others, no exporting or mirroring repository content, and
no sharing code or screenshots from it with third parties.

**4. Term**
The obligations apply during the staff relationship and for **one (1) year**
after it ends. For information covered by section 1(d) (player data), the
obligations continue for as long as the information relates to identifiable
persons.

**5. Intellectual property**
Everything the Staff Member creates for the projects in the context of staff
work — code, textures, models, builds, drafts, concepts — belongs to the Owner.
The Staff Member assigns these rights to the Owner and waives moral rights to
the extent permitted by law. The Owner may provide credit (name or handle) on
the website or Discord as goodwill; credit is not an obligation.

**6. End of the staff relationship**
Within seven (7) days of the end of the staff relationship, or earlier on
request, the Staff Member returns or permanently deletes all Confidential
Information in their possession (downloads, copies, screenshots, spreadsheets)
and confirms this in writing. The Owner may end all access (repositories,
tools, channels) at any time.

**7. Penalty clause**
Each breach of this Agreement entitles the Owner to a penalty of **€ 500
(five hundred euros)**, payable immediately, without prejudice to the Owner’s
right to claim full compensation for the damage suffered and to demand
immediate cessation of the breach. Both Parties ask a court, if called upon to
assess the penalty, to honour the Agreement as written.

**8. Minors**
If the Staff Member is under 18, this Agreement is only valid after a parent
or legal guardian countersigns below.

**9. Final provisions**
This Agreement is governed by Dutch law. Disputes are submitted exclusively to
the court having jurisdiction at the Owner’s place of residence. Amendments
require written agreement. If any provision is held invalid, the remaining
provisions continue in force. Sections 4–6 survive termination.
