## What is a gouw?

A **gouw** (Dutch for "county") is a piece of land you claim on the server — one chunk at a time. Inside your gouw, only you and the people you trust can build, open containers or fight. Gouw is the in-house replacement for the old Lands plugin, and every Lands claim was carried over automatically the first time Gouw started.

Head over to [Gouw — Land Claims](/wiki?page=gouw) to see how protection works, or check the [Commands](/wiki?page=gouw-commands) page to get started with `/gouw claim`.
