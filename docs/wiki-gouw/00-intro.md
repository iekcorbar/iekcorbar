## What is a gouw?

A **gouw** (Dutch for "county") is a piece of land you claim on the server — one chunk at a time. Inside a gouw, only the people it belongs to can build, open containers or fight. Gouw is the in-house replacement for the old Lands plugin, and every Lands claim was carried over automatically the first time Gouw started.

Since version 0.4 a gouw is also a little **town**: the owner is the **mayor**, the people they invite are **residents**, and trusted residents can be promoted to **officers**. Towns share a **bank**, can **buy extra chunk space** with it, and the mayor hands out **rights per resident** (build, chests, doors, mobs, pvp, claim).
