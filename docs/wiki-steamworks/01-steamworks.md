## Steamworks - machines that turn

Steamworks adds steampunk and Japanese-style machines to Iekcorbar: shafts and cogwheels that carry rotation, power sources, processing machines, conveyor belts, a programmable robot arm, trains, custom ores and an oil industry. You only need the normal game - no mods.

### How it works in one minute

* **Rotation.** A power source (hand crank, water wheel) turns. Everything connected to it - shafts, cogwheels, gearboxes, machines - turns with it. Right-click any part with an empty hand to see its speed.
* **Speed (RPM).** Cogwheels change speed: a small cogwheel driven by a large one turns twice as fast. Faster machines work quicker.
* **Stress (Tq).** Every machine puts a load on its network; every source gives capacity. If the load is bigger than the capacity, the whole network **jams**: it stops and puffs steam until you add power or remove a machine. A Stress Gauge shows how close you are.
* **Too fast.** A part that would turn faster than 192 RPM snaps its breaker pin and drops as an item.
* **Opposite directions.** Two sources turning against each other, or gears locking each other in a loop, also jam the network.

### Getting started

1. Craft a few **Shafts** (2 copper ingots, stacked) and a **Hand Crank**.
2. Place a **Millstone**, a shaft under it and the crank under the shaft (see Processing Machines).
3. Hold right-click on the crank. Put cobblestone in the millstone and watch it become gravel.
4. Build a **Water Wheel** in flowing water for power that never stops.

> Picking parts up: left-click a part twice (once in creative). Parts are protected like normal blocks.
