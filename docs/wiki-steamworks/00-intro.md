## Welcome to Steamworks

Iekcorbar is a steampunk world with a Japanese heart, and Steamworks is how it gets its machines. Everything here is built from the normal game: no mods, no downloads, nothing to install. You just place parts and watch them turn.

### What you will do

* **Make things turn.** Start with a Hand Crank, then move on to a Water Wheel, a Windmill or a Steam Engine. Shafts, cogwheels and gearboxes carry the rotation wherever you want it.
* **Put the rotation to work.** Grind grain, press metal, crush ore, brew in a kettle, drill and saw, move items on belts, or teach a Karakuri Arm to do a job for you.
* **Go further.** Lay track and run your own trains, mine new ores (zinc, tin, sulfur), strike oil and refine it, and build with 32 new steampunk and Japanese blocks.

### How the pages are organised

Read the pages in order if you are new: first **Power and Transmission** (every machine needs it), then **Processing Machines**. The rest you can pick up when you need it. Every machine has its own page, so you can also look up one part at a time.

### Good to know first

* Machines **share the rotation** of one network. Too much load and the network stops by itself, it never breaks your parts.
* Steamworks works together with our other plugins: Kitchen uses its flour and mixer, Kura storage can feed belts and arms, and Zukan (the recipe book) shows every recipe.
* Stuck? Right-click any part with an empty hand to see its speed and what is wrong.
* In-game there is the **Engineer's Manual**: type `/sw manual` anywhere for a living guide with clickable contents, recipe links and your own build progress.

### Start here

1. Read [Steamworks](/wiki?page=steamworks) for the one-minute explanation and the first build.
2. Open [Power and Transmission](/wiki?page=steamworks-power) and build your first crank.
3. Look up recipes in [Crafting Recipes](/wiki?page=steamworks-recipes) or with **/zukan** in the game.
