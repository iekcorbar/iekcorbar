## Wildlife - shy animals to find and record

Iekcorbar's countryside has new animals living next to the familiar ones: deer in the forests, mallards on the rivers, fireflies over the swamps at night, crabs scuttling along the beach - and in the east, tanuki at dusk, cranes standing in the reeds, koi gliding under the surface and macaques chattering in the trees. They are **rare and shy**, and each species only lives in its own kind of place. Nothing vanilla was removed - cows, pigs and sheep are all still there.

### How it works

1. **Get a spyglass** (copper + amethyst shard, as in vanilla).
2. **Find an animal** in the right biome. Some only come out by day, fireflies only at night, and snails like the rain. Shy species are also rarer in the wild.
3. **Approach like a naturalist, not a hunter.** Deer, zebras, cranes and songbirds bolt when an upright or sprinting player gets close; a **sneaking, still** one may come near enough. Tortoises hide, hedgehogs curl - even from a friendly boot.
4. **Watch it through the spyglass** until the dots fill. A calm species takes a second; a shy one up to four - and **from far away it goes twice as fast** while standing right in front of it takes twice as long. A bolted animal resets the meter.
5. **Read your journal** with **/wildlife journal**, or craft a **Field Journal** (book + feather + wheat seeds) and right-click it. Species you haven't found yet show a hint - recorded ones show a **true fact** and a **myth** about the animal. Some of those facts surprise even old hands.

### Rare looks

Now and then an animal has a rare colouring - an albino deer, a blue morpho butterfly, a capybara wearing a yuzu. Spotting one gives its own (hidden) advancement and a mark in your journal.

### Advancements

Open the advancement screen and look for the **Wildlife** tab: one advancement per species, one per rare look, and milestones for 5 species, 10 species, every species, your first rare find and 5 rare finds. There is also a separate **Zoo** tab with advancements for building your own zoo: placing props, building entrances, enclosures, comfort areas, paths and decorations.

### Commands

* **/wildlife** - open the Wildlife menu: every species (found ones show details and a click points you to the nearest one), your totals, and shortcuts to the journal, your dragons and the zoo help.
* **/wildlife journal** - read your Field Journal.
* **/wildlife stats [player]** - see how many species you have recorded.
* **/wildlife list [filter]** - list all species and where they live.
* **/wildlife find <species>** - find the nearest animal of that species.
* **/wildlife tamed** - list your tamed dragons.
* **/wildlife spawn <species> [rare] [baby]** - spawn an animal (staff only).
* **/wildlife egg <species> [player]** - give a spawn egg (staff only).
* **/wildlife give <player> <item>** - give an item (staff only).
* **/wildlife info** - server info (staff only).
* **/wildlife reload** - reload config (staff only).
* **/wildlife remove <radius>** - remove animals in a radius (staff only).

### Zoo-builder

Build your own zoo with craftable decor props: doors, toilets, cobblestone paths, welcome/exit signs, flower boxes, picnic tables, parasols, first aid posts and brick walls. Place ticket kiosks, turnstiles and gates to create a working zoo with tickets. Animals can be kept on leads or with name tags. Statues of every animal can be crafted from stone and the animal's drop item.

### Materials from the animals

Adult animals that did not starve can leave a raw material behind: **ivory** (elephant), **antler** (deer), **bear pelt**, **crocodile hide**, **striped hide** (zebra), **kangaroo hide**, **ostrich**, **crane** and **macaw** plumes, **penguin down**, **rhino horn**, **koi scale**, **orca** and **piranha teeth**, and **brass scrap** from a steam dragon. A rare look drops double. Brewers use the koi scale in the Sakagura *Golden Koi Sake*; the Kitchen and Sakagura recognise all of them as ingredients.

### Feeding in the zoo

Only animals that belong to someone (a name tag or a lead) get hungry; wild animals find their own food. Put food in a **feeding trough** (one kind at a time, up to 32): every few seconds it feeds the hungriest animal within six blocks that actually eats that food, and never one that is already well fed. Hunger is remembered across restarts. A starving kept animal slows down and drops nothing.

### Graveyard spawning

Wraiths now spawn at night near graveyards (soul sand/soul soil clusters). They are sfeer-wezen: they drift, shed mist, moan softly and vanish at dawn. They are not in the Field Journal and cannot be bred.

### Behaviour worth knowing

* **Deer and lizards** run when you come close - sneak to get near.
* **Hedgehogs and snails** curl up or hide in their shell; **tortoises** pull in their head and legs.
* **Brown bears** are calm until you hurt them, sleep at night, eat sweet berries - and by day you may see one sit at the riverbank, fish a **salmon** out of the water and eat it on the spot.
* **Wild boars** charge when they feel cornered.
* **Butterflies** pollinate: they flutter over your wheat, carrots, potatoes and beetroots and ripen them a step faster, and flower patches near them spread themselves to bare grass. A field with butterflies visibly outgrows one without.
* **Hunter's trophy**: an animal you have **recorded in your Field Journal first** sometimes leaves its own **statue** as a trophy when you take it down (rarer animals, bigger chance). The naturalist's deal: observe, then hunt.
* **Capybaras** sometimes give a duck or a songbird a ride.
* **Lizards** can drop their tail when hit (it grows back). **Crabs** sometimes drop a claw: hold a **Crab Claw** in your off hand to reach one block further.
* A **name tag** keeps an animal around; unnamed wildlife wanders off when nobody is near. So does a **lead**: an animal you put on a lead stays.
* Only adult bears and boars ever fight back. Every other animal is harmless, and so are their young.
* **Young animals** exist too, and you can raise them: see [Breeding and Babies](/wiki?page=wildlife-breeding).
