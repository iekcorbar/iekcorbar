## Welcome to the Wildlife

Walk a little slower and look around. Thirty new animals now live in Iekcorbar, next to the cows and sheep you already know. They are shy and rare, and each one has its own home: red deer in the forests, mallards on the river, fireflies over the swamp at night, crabs on the beach, elephants and zebras on the savanna - and in the bamboo thickets and snowy woods, the tanuki, the red-crowned crane, the golden koi and troops of macaques. New since spring: roos hopping the plains, an ostrich that outruns you, crocodiles that are logs with opinions, napping koalas, molehills (and once, a mole), rhinos in the dust, penguin colonies on the ice, a blow out in the deep ocean - orca - and red-bellied piranha shoals in the warm shallows, while scarlet macaws scream over the jungle canopy.

This is not about catching or fighting. It is about **finding, watching and recording**, like a naturalist with a field journal. No mods needed.

### What you will do

* **Explore.** Different biomes, different animals. Some come out only by day, some only at night, snails like rain.
* **Watch.** Look at an animal through a spyglass for a moment and it is written in your Field Journal.
* **Collect.** Each species is an advancement in the Wildlife tab, and some animals have a rare colouring that counts as a special find.
* **Be gentle.** Deer run if you walk up to them, bears sleep at night and are calm until hurt. Learn how each animal behaves.

### How the pages are organised

**Wildlife** explains the journal and the advancements. **Species** lists every animal and where to look for it. **Breeding and Babies** explains how to raise young animals, and **Statues** how to build a stone version of any animal.

### Good to know first

* Nothing from the normal game was removed or replaced. Wildlife is only added.
* Animals are scarce on purpose. If you see nothing, keep exploring and try another time of day.
* A **name tag** keeps an animal in place, otherwise it wanders off when nobody is near.

### Start here

1. Craft a spyglass (copper ingot and amethyst shard).
2. Read [Wildlife](/wiki?page=wildlife) to see how the journal works.
3. Pick an animal from [Species](/wiki?page=wildlife-species) and go and find it.
