## Animal statues

Every animal can be placed as a **true-size statue**, in three versions: the animal, its **rare look** and a **baby**. They are decoration for your zoo or garden.

### Crafting

Craft them on a crafting table, no pattern needed. Use stone and the **item that animal drops** (the same one shown on its advancement):

| Statue | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Adult | 3 stone + the animal's item |
| Baby | 2 stone + the animal's item |
| Rare | 3 stone + the animal's item + 1 amethyst shard |

For example a deer statue is 3 stone and leather.

### Placing and moving

* Right-click a block with the statue to place it. It turns to face you.
* **Hit it a few times** to pick it up again.
* The elephant statue is three blocks tall, so leave some room.

Statues stand still; the real animals are found in the wild. They go well with the **Zoo** props: fences, gates, troughs and info boards.
