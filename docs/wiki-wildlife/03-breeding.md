## Breeding and babies

Every animal can have young - even the bees and the songbirds. Raising them is slow on purpose.

### How to breed

1. **Right-click an adult with its food** (table below). Hearts appear: it is in love for 30 seconds.
2. Do the same to **a second animal of the same kind** within a few blocks.
3. The two walk to each other and **a baby appears**. Both parents need about 5 minutes before they can have another one.

If a lot of the same animal is already close by, the game says it is **too crowded** and nothing happens. That keeps farms small.

### Babies

A baby is **half the size**, with a big head, and it is harmless - even a bear cub or a boar piglet. It grows up after about 20 minutes. Feeding a baby its food makes it grow a little faster.

### Keep a baby small

Give a baby a **golden dandelion** and it **stays a baby**. Give it another golden dandelion and it starts growing again. A baby kept small is yours: it stays where you left it, even when nobody is around.

### Keeping your animals

Wild animals wander off when nobody is near. A **name tag**, a **lead**, or a golden dandelion (for babies) keeps an animal for good. Perfect for a zoo.

### Good to know

* A baby born next to its parents looks like them. Rare parents have a good chance of a rare baby.
* A grown-up that was on a lead slips out of it and the lead drops to the ground.
* Young animals now and then show up on their own in a bigger group.
