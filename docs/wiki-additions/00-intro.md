## What is this?

Steamworks Additions is a small sister plugin of Steamworks: **atmosphere props** for the towns, breweries and stations. No rotation, no stress - just brass, copper and a bell that knows what time it is. It runs next to Steamworks but never needs a shaft.

### The props

* **Steam Clock** - a brass tower clock with a floating time plate that reads the real wall clock and strikes its bell on every hour.
* **Brass Railing** - iron posts with brass bars that join each other automatically. Queue dividers, quay edges, garden borders.
* **Vent Grille** - a copper vent with green slats. On a wall or free-standing, it tells everyone that something steams behind this facade.

### In the game

* Every prop has a workbench recipe (see the [recipes page](/recipes) or `/zukan`), shows up in your recipe book, and is picked back up by left-clicking it (twice by default).
* Staff hand them out with `/sa give <player> <prop>`; `/sa list` shows what exists.
* The props save themselves in the chunk, exactly like Steamworks machines: reload a chunk, the prop is back on its spot.

### Good to know

* The clock reads the *world* clock (a Minecraft day is 20 minutes), so it strikes every 50 real-time minutes. It only makes noise and ticks while players are nearby - an empty quarter is silent and costs nothing.
* Nothing here touches the Steamworks network. You can run Additions without a single shaft in sight (though a clock on top of a pumping station does look fantastic).
