## Brass Railing

Iron posts with two brass bars. The railing **joins its neighbours automatically**: place a second one next to it and the bars grow into one run; corners, T-junctions and end posts pick the right piece by themselves - exactly like the garden fence, only with more brass.

* Walk over it: it is low (you bump into the posts, but you can step along).
* Craft: **3 Iron Ingots + 2 Sticks** → **4 railing pieces**.
* Classic uses: queue lines at the station and ticket kiosk, quay edges, the brewery terrace, keeping the horses out of the flower beds.

## Vent Grille

A copper frame with three green-brass slats over a dark opening, with bolts in the corners.

* **Mounts on any wall** - right-click a wall face with the item and it hangs flat, facing outward. On the ground it leans against the block you placed it on.
* Craft: **2 Copper Ingots + 2 Iron Nuggets** → 1 grille.
* It is decoration that tells a story: put one above a kettle line, on a boiler wall or along the brewery's back street, and everyone knows where the steam comes from. (It does not connect to real pipes - that would be too easy.)

## Taking them back

Left-click twice like every prop. Railing sections come apart per piece: take one from the middle and the neighbours re-shape themselves the next time they load.

Staff: `/sa give <player> brass_railing|vent_grille`.
