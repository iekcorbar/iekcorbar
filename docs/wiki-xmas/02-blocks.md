## The eleven seasonal blocks

All of them craft in a normal 3x3 grid, are solid walkable blocks (the street lamp is two blocks tall) and break with two punches in survival. "A+B+C" means one of each, in any arrangement unless noted.

| Block | Recipe | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| Pressed Snow Block | 2x Snow Block + Snowball + Gunpowder | 4 |
| Clear Ice Block | 2x Ice + Glass Pane + Quartz (glows faintly) | 4 |
| Pine Log | 2x Spruce Log + Hay Bale | 4 |
| Pine Planks | Stripped Spruce Log + 2x Spruce Planks | 8 |
| Roof Tile | Red Terracotta + Bricks + Snowball | 4 |
| Lit Window Block | Glass Pane + Lantern + 2x Spruce Planks (light level 12) | 2 |
| Gift Cube (Red) | 2x Red Wool + 2x Gold Nugget | 4 |
| Gift Cube (Green) | 2x Green Wool + 2x Gold Nugget | 4 |
| Candy Cane Block | 2x Sugar + Red Dye + White Dye | 4 |
| Gingerbread Block | 3x Cookie + Egg | 4 |
| Village Street Lamp | Iron Ingot + 2x Stick + Lantern + Snowball (2 blocks tall, light level 14) | 1 |

### Good to know

* Breaking a Christmas block is two punches in survival (one click in creative or for staff) and the block drops back as an item.
* `/xmas list` shows the catalogue in game; the blocks also appear in `/zukan`.
