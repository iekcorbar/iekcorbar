## The village

The Xmas Town district is a Christmas village built from the seasonal blocks plus the relic props. It grows in parts:

* **The plaza** - the heart, with the clock tower and a free lane kept open for the countdown board.
* **The Great Tree** - a Christmas tree as tall as a small house, ringed by lights.
* **The red hut** and the **row of cottages** - pine walls, roof tiles and lit windows; in the evenings half the street glows.
* **The market street** - stalls with ginger, greens and gold, the place to meet other players during the season.
* **The lantern tower** - village street lamps, two blocks tall, marking the path in the snow.

### Daily life

* **Santa walks the plaza** during the season (see [Santa](/wiki?page=xmas-santa)). He never hurts anyone; if you try to trade with him he just greets you.
* **The countdown board** on the plaza ticks down to December 24. Right-click it with a time you live in - it remembers the setting.
* Placed Christmas blocks and props keep working all year; most of the fun (gifts, advent doors, the countdown) follows the real calendar.

### Good to know

* Christmas props with a use (sleigh, gift box, advent calendar, music box, snow globe, cookie plate) are **Relics** - see the Relics chapter for what each one does.
* The village is placed by staff piece by piece. A part that is not there yet will simply appear later; nothing you built nearby breaks.
