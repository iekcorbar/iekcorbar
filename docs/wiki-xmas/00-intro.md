## Welcome to Xmas Town

Iekcorbar does Christmas properly: a festive village district, craftable Christmas building blocks and a Santa who actually walks around and hands out presents. Everything is built into the normal game - no mods, no gimmicks.

### What you will do

* **Visit the village.** A plaza with a clock tower, a huge Christmas tree, cottages, a market street and lantern towers. Staff place new parts of it over the season, so every week it looks different.
* **Meet Santa.** He wanders the plaza, his bell rings when you come close, and during **24-26 December he gives every player one present per day**. Find him before he walks back into the snow.
* **Build for the season.** Eleven Christmas building blocks - pressed snow, clear ice, pine, roof tiles, lit windows, gift cubes, candy cane, gingerbread and a two-block street lamp - all craftable in a normal crafting table.
* **Count down.** The countdown board on the plaza shows the time until Christmas in your own timezone (right-click it to switch).

### How the pages are organised

**Xmas Town** explains the village and its daily life, **Christmas Blocks** lists every craftable block with its recipe, and **Santa** tells you how and when he gives gifts. The decorative props - sleigh, advent calendar, music box, snow globe and friends - live in the **Relics** chapter, because they are relics.
