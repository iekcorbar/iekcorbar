## Seasons

The Sakagura garden follows the seasons. Every 7 real days, the season changes, affecting which crops grow and how fast.

### The four seasons

| Season | Duration | Growing crops | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Spring** | 7 days | Cherry, Ume, Peach, Strawberry, Apricot, Mikan, Kiwi, Tomato, Raspberry, Gooseberry | Cherry blossoms and fresh growth |
| **Summer** | 7 days | Yuzu, Mango, Papaya, Guava, Rambutan, Longan, Mangosteen, Dragon Fruit, Passion Fruit, Vanilla, Chili, Maize, Tomato | Lush growth and tropical fruits |
| **Autumn** | 7 days | Grape, Apple, Pear, Persimmon, Loquat, Pomegranate, Fig, Quince, Olive, Date, Lychee, Elderberry, Cranberry, Mulberry, Barley, Rye, Hops | Harvest time and falling leaves |
| **Winter** | 7 days | Ginger, Wasabi, Daikon, Rice, Juniper, Blueberry, Blackcurrant, Strawberry | Dormant gardens and hardy crops |

### How it works

- **Seasons advance automatically** every 7 real days (configurable). A broadcast announces the change.
- **In-season crops** grow at normal speed.
- **Out-of-season crops** grow 30-70% slower (they're not meant for this season).
- **Wild spawns** only include crops for the current season.
- **Admins** can force a season change with `/brew season set <spring|summer|autumn|winter>`.

### Commands

| Command | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| `/brew season status` | Everyone | Show current season and time until next |
| `/brew season list` | Everyone | List all seasons with their crops |
| `/brew season crops` | Everyone | Show which crops grow this season |
| `/brew season set <season>` | Admin | Force a season change |

### Tips

- **Plan ahead** — plant crops that will be in-season soon
- **Winter is for hardy crops** — ginger, wasabi, daikon, rice, and juniper thrive
- **Seasonal drinks** — some recipes taste better with seasonal ingredients
- **Competition themes** — admins can run themed competitions per season
