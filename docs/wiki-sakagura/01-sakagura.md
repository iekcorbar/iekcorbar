## Sakagura - brewing

Brew sake, shochu, whisky, wine, gin, tea and much more. The better you follow a recipe, the better the drink: up to five stars. You only need the normal game - no mods.

### How to brew

1. **Cauldron.** Fill a cauldron with water. Put **heat under it** (fire, campfire, lava, magma or a lit furnace) to **cook**, or leave it cold to **mix**.
2. **Ingredients.** Right-click the cauldron with each ingredient (one per click). Right-click with an empty hand to see what's inside and for how long it has been going.
3. **Wait.** Every recipe has its own time in real minutes - too short or too long lowers the quality.
4. **Bottle it.** Right-click with a **glass bottle**: one cauldron gives three bottles.
5. **Distill** (some recipes): put the bottles and a **glowstone dust** in a **Steam Still** and give it **heat from below**. Every round uses one glowstone and is one distill run.
6. **Age** (some recipes): put the bottles in a **Sake Cask**. They age in real time - also while you're away. One year is 20 minutes. Many recipes want a certain wood.

A bottle that isn't finished yet says so ("Unfinished Sake - Next: age it 2 years in spruce"), so you always know the next step. A brew that matches nothing turns into a **Murky Brew**.

### Steam Still

Craft a still from a barrel, 2 iron ingots, blaze powder and a glowstone dust (all together in any order in a crafting grid). Put the bottles inside, add **glowstone dust** and place **fire, lava, a campfire or a lit furnace under it**: every 20 seconds one glowstone is spent and the bottles inside get one distill run. A hopper can feed bottles in and collect them, so the still fits in a line. Right-clicking the still tells you what it is doing.

### Sake Casks

Craft a cask from 6 planks of one wood and 2 iron nuggets (planks around, nuggets top and bottom in the middle). There is one for every wood - oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, cherry, mangrove, bamboo - and a steampunk **copper cask** (copper ingots and iron ingots). A cask works like a barrel: hoppers can fill and empty it.

### Drinking

Drinks give their effects - better quality, stronger good effects and milder bad ones. Alcohol makes you **drunk**: you stumble, the world sways, your chat gets slurred, too much makes you throw up, and if you overdo it you pass out and wake up at your spawn point with a hangover. Check yourself with **/brew drunk**.
