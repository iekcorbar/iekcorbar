## Orchard Trees

Sakagura 0.5.0 added real trees to the garden. A sapling is planted with a right-click on grass or dirt (not farmland - trees take a spot of their own) and grows in three stages: a sprout, a young tree and a full tree. Each step takes about twenty minutes of real time; bone meal on the trunk skips one.

### The five species

| Tree | Fruit | Grown from | Good to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sakura | 2-4 Cherries | Cherry + 2 Sticks | Pink blossom; petals drift off the canopy |
| Ume Plum | 2-3 Ume Plums | Ume Plums + 2 Sticks | The sake fruit, off a tree instead of a bush |
| Apple | 2-4 Apples | Apples + 2 Sticks | A classic, with bigger yields than the bush |
| Orange | 2-4 Mikan Oranges | Mikan Oranges + 2 Sticks | Glossy green canopy |
| Red Maple | none | 2 Red Mushrooms + 2 Sticks | Pure decoration - golden sparkles in autumn colours |
| Cherry | 2-4 Cherries | Cherries + 2 Sticks | A smaller, denser canopy than the sakura |
| Peach | 2-3 Peaches | Peaches + 2 Sticks | Pink blossoms, fuzzy fruit |
| Lemon | 2-4 Yuzu | Yuzu + 2 Sticks | Glossy dark canopy, tart fruit |
| Fig | 2-3 Figs | Figs + 2 Sticks | Broad leaves, sweet purple fruit |
| Walnut | none | Olives + 2 Sticks | Pure decoration - broad canopy, no fruit |

### Picking and felling

* A ripe tree shows fruit dots in its top canopy. Right-click the trunk and 2-4 pieces of fruit drop - the canopy then ripens again in about twenty minutes.
* Punch the trunk three times and the tree comes down; you get the sapling back (plus any fruit that was hanging). Trees need four blocks of air above them and a little space from their neighbours.
* The fruit is the same fruit the bush version gives, so every drink recipe works with tree fruit too - and vice versa.

### Saplings

One craft: the tree's own fruit (or two red mushrooms for the maple) plus two sticks in any arrangement. Fruiting trees can also be bonemealed as saplings to speed them up. Wild trees do not spawn - an orchard is something you plant.
