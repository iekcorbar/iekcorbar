## Welcome to the Sakagura

A sakagura is a Japanese sake brewery, and ours is open to everyone. Brew sake, shochu, whisky, wine, gin, tea and many more drinks, and grow the barley, hops, grapes and fruit they need. A good brewer follows the recipe and is rewarded with up to five stars. No mods needed.

### What you will do

* **Grow.** Forty-eight garden crops - from barley and hops to yuzu, mangoes and dragon fruit - plus five orchard trees, wild or on your own farmland.
* **Brew.** A cauldron with heat cooks, a cold one mixes. Add the ingredients, wait the right number of real minutes, and bottle the result.
* **Distill and age.** Some drinks need a Steam Still, and others years in a cask. Casks age in real time, even while you are offline.
* **Enjoy (carefully).** Drinks give effects, but alcohol also makes you drunk. Drink too much and you pass out and wake up with a hangover.

### How the pages are organised

**Sakagura** has the step-by-step brewing guide. **Garden** lists the crops and where they grow, and **Recipes** has every drink with its steps. The [Sakagura guide](/sakagura) on this website explains each step in plain language, with a quality calculator.

### Good to know first

* A bottle always tells you its next step, so you never lose track.
* Some recipes use Kitchen ingredients or Steamworks plates, so the plugins work together.
* Not sure what a drink needs? **/zukan** shows every recipe with its steps.

### Start here

1. Read [Sakagura](/wiki?page=sakagura) and brew your first drink in a cauldron.
2. Plant some crops from the [Garden](/wiki?page=sakagura-garden).
3. Pick a drink from the [Recipes](/wiki?page=sakagura-recipes) and aim for five stars.
