## What is this?

Steamworks Power is the industry add-on of Steamworks: five machines that make a factory out of a river and a lot of patience. They run on the same rotation network as every other Steamworks part - same shafts, same stress, same wrench.

### The machines

* **Water Turbine** - a brass drum that sits in river current and turns it into strong, steady rotation. The quiet cousin of the water wheel: less noise, more torque.
* **Mechanical Press** - a brass arch that stamps whatever lies on top of it. Nine zinc nuggets become a clean ingot; four snow blocks become dense ice. The press does what the furnace can't.
* **Stock Ticker** - a small copper counter that leans against a chest or barrel and shows, on a floating plate, what is inside and how much of it.
* **Brass Flywheel** - a heavy wheel on the line. It winds itself up while the power runs; when the power falls away it takes over and coasts the machines through the dip, until its charge runs out.
* **Sieve Funnel** - a funnel with a brass mesh at the end of a belt line. Show it one item and only that item falls into the container below; everything else is tossed off the side.

### In the game

* All five craft at the workbench and show up in `/zukan` and the [recipes page](/recipes).
* They need rotation, not fuel: hook a turbine or steam engine up to the network and the press works while the network runs.
* Staff hand out parts with `/swpower give <player> <machine>`; `/swpower list` shows what exists.

### Good to know

* The press is a working surface: items land on top, the ram stamps them, the results drop beside it. A funnel or hopper minecart underneath keeps the line running.
* The stock ticker does not rotate - it reads containers. Place it against a chest, barrel, or any Steamworks machine that holds items.
* The water turbine wants its full drum surrounded: water touching the sides, the shaft going through the ends. In a millrace that is exactly what you build anyway.
* The flywheel doesn't care which source charges it - river, wind or steam. Right-click it to see how full it is. It only takes over when the line actually stops.
* The sieve funnel sorts items, not fluids. Right-click it empty to check its filter, sneak right-click empty to open it up again.
