## Welcome to Kura

Every long-time player knows the problem: ten chests, three barrels and no idea where the iron went. Kura (Japanese for "storehouse") fixes that. It joins your chests into one network, so a single terminal shows everything you own, with search and sorting. No mods needed, it all works in the normal game.

### What you will do

* **Connect.** Run an Inventory Cable beside your chests. A cable joins whatever it touches, and so does every container touching those - that is the whole rule.
* **Find and fetch.** Open a Storage Terminal, search for an item and take it out. Put things back by clicking them in.
* **Craft from storage.** The Crafting Terminal uses what is in your network and refills its grid after each craft.
* **Reach it from anywhere.** Wireless terminals open your network from across the base, or even from another world with the advanced version.
* **Automate.** Hoppers, filters and level emitters keep machines supplied. They also work with Steamworks belts and arms.

### How the pages are organised

**Parts** explains every block you can place, **Tools** the items you carry (filters, configurator, wireless terminals, Paint Kit). **Recipes** shows how to craft them and **Automation** covers hoppers and redstone. Every part and tool has its own page.

### Good to know first

* Sake casks from Sakagura are never used as storage, so your drinks keep ageing.
* Furnaces, hoppers and brewing stands are not storage either.
* Kura talks to **/zukan**: from a Crafting Terminal you can look up a recipe and fill the grid from your network.
