## Kura - one terminal for all your chests

Kura joins your chests into one storage network. A **Storage Terminal** then shows every item in every connected chest on one screen, with search and sorting. Take things out, put things in, craft straight from storage, and reach it from across your base with a wireless terminal. You only need the normal game - no mods.

### How it works in one minute

1. **Place an Inventory Connector** and put chests, barrels or shulker boxes against it. Chests touching those chests join too.
2. **Place a Storage Terminal** against the connector (or against any chest in the network).
3. **Right-click the terminal.** Every item in the network is listed with its total amount.
4. **Click** an item to take a stack, **right-click** for half, **shift-click** to send it to your inventory. Click with an item on your cursor (or shift-click in your own inventory) to store it.

Gaps between chests? Fill them with **Inventory Trim**. Chests far away? Run **Inventory Cable** from the connector and put a **Cable Connector** on each chest.
