## Zukan - find any item and how to make it

Zukan is the server's recipe book. It lists **every item** in the game - vanilla and everything our plugins add - and shows how to **make** it and what it is **used for**. No more guessing which machine does what.

### Open it

* Type **/zukan** (or /recipes, /jei).
* Craft the **Zukan book** (a book and a compass, anywhere) and right-click it, or press **F** (swap hands) while holding it.
* **/zukan <words>** opens the list already searched, for example /zukan copper.
* Hold an item and type **/zukan hand** for its recipes, or **/zukan uses** to see what it is used for.

The browser has a steampunk look (brass frame, cogs and pipes). It is drawn by the server resource pack, so it opens as its own screen - the normal inventory key (E) cannot be extended by a server.

### The item list

* **Click** an item: how it is made. **Right-click**: what it is used for.
* **Shift-click** an item: bookmark it. The bookmark button at the bottom shows only your bookmarks.
* **Search** (the compass): type words for an item name, **#logs** for an item group, **@steamworks** for one plugin's items.
* The **Show** button switches between everything, vanilla, and each plugin.

### Recipes

Each item can have several recipe types, shown as tabs along the top: crafting, smelting, brewing, and one tab for each of our machines (millstone, steam hammer, crushing wheels, kettle and mixer, cooking pot, cutting board, Sakagura brewing, garden crops and so on).

* The little **station** icon under a recipe shows where it is made. Click it like any other item.
* A slot that can hold several items (for example "any planks") **cycles** through the options on its own.
* Use the arrows to flip through several recipes of one type. **Back** returns to the item you came from.
* Notes on the recipe (cooking time, chance of an extra output, heat needed) are in the paper icon.

### With Kura

Standing at a Kura **Crafting Terminal**, press the **Recipes** button to look up an item. On a crafting recipe, **Move ingredients** takes what you need out of the storage network and fills the crafting grid for you.

### Staff

* **/zukan reload** rebuilds the list after you change plugins or recipes.
* Staff with the cheat permission get a **Cheat mode** button: click an item to take it.

**Recipes on the website**

All Steamworks, Kitchen and Kura recipes are also listed on the website: **Play > Recipes**. That page has a search and a type filter, and a short guide to Zukan.
