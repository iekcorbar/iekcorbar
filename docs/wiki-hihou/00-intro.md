## BedWars

Protect your team's bed. Break theirs. That is the whole war.

BedWars runs on the dedicated arena islands (built from the `bedwars-*` schematics): every team gets a home island with a **bed**, two **iron generators** and one **gold generator**, a shop marker and a team chest. Diamond and emerald islands float between the teams - those are the mid-game fights.

**Getting in**

* `/ih bw list` — the arenas on the server and who is waiting.
* `/ih bw join <arena>` — stand in the lobby circle and wait. When enough players are in, a 10-second countdown starts and the islands open.
* `/ih bw kit` — pick your kit before the drop (see [Kits](/wiki?page=hihou-kits)).

**Winning and losing**

* With your bed alive you respawn on your island after a 5-second death timer.
* Lose the bed and every death is permanent - you flip to spectator and watch through the sidebar.
* The last team with a live bed (or just live players) wins, followed by a 15-second fireworks show in the team colours before everyone is handed back to the survival world.
* Nobody is winning fast enough? The clock decides: at 25 minutes every remaining bed crumbles (sudden death), and at 40 minutes the game is called.

During a match a **sidebar** replaces your normal tab list: teams, beds, the match clock, the mode and your own purse of ore. It leaves again the moment you leave the game.
