## In the match

**Ore** is money. The pads on your island print it while the match runs:

| Generator | Drops | Every | Up to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iron (×2 per team) | 2 iron | 2 s | 32 lying around |
| Gold | 2 gold | 6 s | 7 lying around |
| Diamond (mid) | 1 diamond | 30 s → 20 s at 6 min → 15 s at 18 min | 4 |
| Emerald (mid) | 1 emerald | 70 s → 50 s at 12 min → 30 s at 24 min | 4 |

You pay with the ore you physically carry - pick it up off the pad or spend it straight from the pile.

**The bed** can only be destroyed by enemies: breaking it, sleeping in it, TNT next to it, or a well-timed **Bed Breach Charge** from the shop. Wool around it is normal; wool *on* it is a crime scene (buy shears).

**Dying** costs 5 seconds and nothing else - your kit and carried ore wait at base as long as the bed stands. If you quit or crash mid-match, `/ih bw rejoin` pulls you back into the same game.

**Spectating your friends**: after elimination, `/ih bw follow <player>` parks you next to anyone still fighting.

**Parties**: `/ih bw party invite <player>` sends a clickable invite; whoever accepts is placed on the same team as you at balance time. `/ih bw party leave` opts out again.

**Team chest**: every island has one that only your team can open during the match. It pays out: when the game finishes, whatever is still inside drops on top of the chest instead of vanishing.

**The sidebar** (in-match tab list) shows teams and their bed status, the clock, the current mode and your own purse. `/ih bw stats` or `/ih bw stats top` work from anywhere and report your season numbers.
