## The shop

Right-click your team's **shop marker** (an anvil at base) or type `/ih bw shop`. Six tabs, paid with the ore you carry - no coins, no shopkeeper, no refund games.

**Blocks**

| Item | Costs |
|---|---|
| White wool ×16 | 4 iron |
| White wool ×64 | 12 iron |
| Glass ×8 | 4 iron |
| Ladder ×16 | 3 gold |
| Oak planks ×32 | 6 iron |

**Armor**

| Item | Costs |
|---|---|
| Leather helmet | 10 iron |
| Chain leggings | 15 iron |
| Chain chestplate | 20 iron |
| Iron helmet | 4 gold |
| Iron chestplate | 3 diamonds |
| Iron leggings | 2 diamonds |

**Weapons**

| Item | Costs |
|---|---|
| Wooden sword | 5 iron |
| Stone sword | 10 iron |
| Stone axe | 12 iron |
| Iron sword | 6 gold |
| Diamond sword | 6 diamonds |
| Bow | 4 gold |
| Arrows ×12 | 2 gold |
| Shears | 6 iron |
| TNT | 3 emeralds |
| Ender pearl | 3 diamonds |
| **Bed Breach Charge** | 8 emeralds |

The breach charge is the siege tool: right-click it within 16 blocks of an enemy bed and that bed is gone - it counts and pays exactly like breaking it by hand.

**Food**: bread ×4 (3 iron), cooked chicken ×4 (2 gold), cooked beef ×4 (3 gold), golden carrots ×2 (5 gold), cake (10 iron).

**Potions**: Healing splash (1 emerald), Fire Resistance (4 gold), Leaping (2 emeralds).

## Team upgrades

The **Upgrades** tab spends **emeralds for the whole team** - bought once, they hold for the rest of the match:

| Upgrade | Levels | Cost per level | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Protection | 3 | 2 / 4 / 6 | Resistance I on the team |
| Sharpness | 3 | 2 / 4 / 7 | Strength on the team |
| Forge | 3 | 3 / 5 / 8 | Your iron gens tick faster and richer |
| Healers | 2 | 4 / 7 | Team-wide Regeneration pulse every 45 s |
| Trap | 2 | 4 / 6 | Intruders within 6 blocks of your spawn are slowed and announced |
| Auto-Earn | 1 | 6 | Your team's iron and gold fly straight into a teammate's pockets |
| Shockwave | 2 | 5 / 8 | Attackers sometimes get blasted off you |
