## Kits

Pick one with `/ih bw kit <name>` - in the lobby or any time during the match. The choice is yours until you change it, and a fresh copy is handed out at **every spawn and respawn**, so dying never leaves you empty-handed.

| Kit | In your hands from the drop |
|---|---|
| **classic** | Stone sword - a fair fight to start the war |
| **builder** | Wooden axe + 16 white wool on the hotbar - bridge first, fight later |
| **miner** | Iron pickaxe - the diamond islands will thank you |
| **archer** | Bow + 12 arrows - keep them honest from 20 blocks |

There is no penalty for swapping mid-match; the next respawn simply hands you the new kit.
