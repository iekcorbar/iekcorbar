## Stations

Stations are placed like blocks. Break them by hitting them a few times (one hit in creative); whatever is inside drops.

### Knives

| Knife | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Flint Knife | flint + stick (diagonal) |
| Iron Hocho | iron ingot + stick (diagonal) |
| Diamond Hocho | diamond + stick (diagonal) |
| Netherite Hocho | Diamond Hocho + netherite ingot in a smithing table |

Knives cut food on the Cutting Board and are light weapons.

### Cutting Board

Recipe: **stick + 2 planks** in a row.

1. Right-click the board with an ingredient to put it on.
2. Right-click with a **knife** to cut it - the pieces pop out. Every cut uses a little knife durability.
3. Right-click with an empty hand to take it back.

### Cooking Pot

Recipe: **iron ingot, stick, iron ingot** on top, **brick, empty, brick** in the middle, **3 bricks** at the bottom.

Put it **on a heat source**: campfire, fire, lava, magma block, a lit furnace or smoker, or a lit Kamado Stove. Right-click to open it:

* **6 ingredient slots** (left) - one item from each filled slot is used per dish.
* **Container slot** - bowls or glass bottles for soups and drinks.
* **Result slot** (right) - take the finished food out.

The clock in the middle tells you what is happening: cooking progress, "Needs heat", "Needs a bowl", or "No recipe with these ingredients". The pot keeps cooking while nobody looks inside, as long as the area is loaded.

### Kamado Stove

Recipe: **3 bricks**, **brick, campfire, brick**, **3 cobblestone**.

* Light it with **flint and steel** (or a fire charge); put it out with a **water bucket** or a **shovel**.
* A lit stove **heats a Cooking Pot** placed on top of it.
* Without a pot on top it is a **grill**: right-click with raw food (anything a campfire can cook) to put up to 4 items on it. They cook twice as fast as on a campfire and pop off when done.
