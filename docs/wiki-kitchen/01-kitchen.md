## Kitchen - Japanese farming and cooking

Kitchen adds new crops, cooking stations and Japanese dishes to Iekcorbar. Grow rice and soybeans, cut fish into sashimi, cook ramen in a clay pot over a fire, and put a sushi platter on the table for your friends to eat from piece by piece. You only need the normal game - no mods.

### How it works in one minute

1. **Find seeds.** Wild crops grow in the world (rice by swamps and rivers, tea in cherry groves and jungles, and so on). Break them for seeds.
2. **Plant them** on farmland like wheat. Rice needs wet farmland (water nearby).
3. **Cut** ingredients on a **Cutting Board** with a **knife**.
4. **Cook** meals in a **Cooking Pot** placed on a campfire, fire, lava, magma block or a lit **Kamado Stove**.
5. **Eat** dishes straight away, or **sneak + right-click** a block to put a meal down and share it.

### Works with Steamworks

* Steamworks **flour** can replace wheat in noodles, soy sauce and tempura.
* A **millstone** grinds rice into flour, a **steam hammer** pounds steamed rice into mochi, and a heated **kettle + mixer** makes tofu and steamed rice in bulk.
