## Welcome to the Kitchen

Kitchen turns farming and food into a hobby of its own. Plant rice in a flooded field, harvest tea in a cherry grove, cut fish on a cutting board and cook a bowl of ramen over a fire. Then do what makes it special: put the dish on the table and let your friends eat from it, bite by bite. Everything works in the normal game, no mods needed.

### What you will do

* **Grow.** Ten new crops grow in the wild and on your farmland: rice, soybeans, napa cabbage, green onion, daikon, tea, corn, tomato, chili peppers and sesame. Picking a ripe crop with a right-click makes it grow back.
* **Prepare.** Use a knife on the Cutting Board, then cook in the Cooking Pot over a campfire or a Kamado Stove.
* **Share.** Sushi platters, ramen, bento boxes, dango and tea sets can be placed on a table. Every bite is its own little animation.
* **Decorate.** Tansu cabinets, baskets, crates, tatami mats and cushions make the kitchen yours.

### How the pages are organised

Start with **Crops** (where to find seeds), then **Stations** (how to cut and cook), then **Recipes** and **Shared Meals**. **Props** is the furniture page. Each crop, station, meal and prop has its own page.

### Good to know first

* Food is more than decoration: dishes give good effects, and a shared meal feeds a whole table.
* Kitchen works with Steamworks (flour, mochi, tofu in bulk), with Sakagura (drinks need Kitchen ingredients) and with Kura (store your crates in the network).
* The recipe book **/zukan** lists every cutting and cooking recipe.
