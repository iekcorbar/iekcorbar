### What the dishes do

* **Miso Soup, Oden, Nabe**: Regeneration for a few seconds.
* **Ramen, Gyudon, Oyakodon**: Regeneration plus Resistance for a minute.
* **Green Tea**: Speed for a minute and cures poison, hunger and nausea. You can always drink it.
* **Fugu Sashimi**: very filling and gives night vision... unless the cook got it wrong (15% poison).
