## Shared Meals

Some dishes are meant to be put on the table. **Sneak + right-click the top of a block** to put one down. Anyone can then **right-click it to take a bite**: the chopsticks pick up one piece, you eat it, and the next person takes the next piece. Tea cups are lifted and drunk the same way.

| Meal | How to make it | Bites |
|---|---|---|
| Sushi Platter | Plate + 2 Salmon Nigiri + 2 Tuna Nigiri + 2 Maki Rolls | 6 |
| Ramen | from the Cooking Pot (you can also just eat it) | 4 |
| Bento Box | wooden slab + Steamed Rice + Tamagoyaki + Kabocha Tempura + Takuan + Salmon Sashimi | 4 |
| Dango Plate | Plate + 3 Dango | 3 |
| Tea Set | Plate + 4 Green Tea | 4 cups |

### Good to know

* You can't take a bite when you're full - except tea: you can always drink tea (each cup gives Speed).
* The last bite of a ramen bowl also gives the ramen's Regeneration and Resistance.
* When the meal is finished the empty plate (or bowl) stays for a moment and then drops.
* **Hit** a meal to pick it up. An untouched meal comes back whole; a started one only gives the plate or bowl back.
* Meals can stand on any solid block, including Kitchen crates and cabinets.
