## Crops

Kitchen crops grow on **farmland** (or [Rich Soil](/wiki?page=kitchen-props)) like wheat: they need light (level 9 or more) and only grow while the area is loaded. Bone meal speeds them up. A ripe crop can be **picked by right-clicking it** - it drops the harvest and starts growing again, so you don't have to replant.

| Crop | Plant | Harvest | Where it grows wild |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rice | Rice | 2-4 Rice | swamps, rivers |
| Soybean | Soybeans | 2-4 Soybeans | plains |
| Napa Cabbage | Napa Cabbage Seeds | 1 Napa Cabbage + seeds | beaches |
| Green Onion | Green Onion | 2-3 Green Onions | forests, taiga |
| Daikon | Daikon Seeds | 1 Daikon + seeds | meadows, flower fields |
| Tea | Tea Seeds | 2-3 Tea Leaves | cherry groves, jungles |
| Corn | Corn | 2-4 Corn | badlands, savanna |
| Tomato | Tomato | 1-3 Tomatoes | - |
| Chili Pepper | Chili Pepper | 1-3 Chili Peppers | - |
| Sesame | Sesame Seeds | 2-3 Sesame Seeds | - |

### Good to know

* **Rice** only grows on **wet** farmland (water within 4 blocks, like normal farmland hydration). On Rich Soil it needs water within 2 blocks.
* **Tea** is a bush: after picking it drops back to a young bush instead of starting from seed.
* **Corn** cuts on the Cutting Board into **Cornmeal** (for polenta and pickling).
* Breaking a young crop gives the seed back. Breaking a ripe one gives the harvest.
* Trampling the farmland pops the crop off, just like wheat.
* With a **knife**, breaking tall grass sometimes gives **Straw** (for bales, mats and compost).
