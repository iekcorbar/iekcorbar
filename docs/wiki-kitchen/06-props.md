## Props

Furniture and storage for your kitchen and farm. Break them by hitting them a few times; what's inside drops.

| Prop | Recipe | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Tansu Cabinet | 3 wooden slabs, trapdoor + empty + trapdoor, 3 wooden slabs | Storage (27 slots), turns to face you |
| Bamboo Basket | bamboo in a U shape (7 bamboo) | Storage (9 slots) that catches items dropped onto it |
| Crates and sacks | 9 of one crop (rice, soybeans, napa cabbage, daikon, green onion, tea leaves, carrots, potatoes, beetroots) | Packed storage block; craft it back into 9 |
| Rice Straw Bale | 9 Straw | Decoration; craft it back into 9 straw |
| Organic Compost | dirt + 2 Straw + 2 Tree Bark + bone meal | Turns into Rich Soil after about 20 minutes (faster with water next to it) |
| Rich Soil | from compost | Kitchen crops planted on it grow faster; no hoe needed |
| Tatami Mat | 3 Straw in a row (gives 2) | Floor mat you can walk over |
| Zabuton Cushion | wool on top of Straw | Right-click to sit on it |
