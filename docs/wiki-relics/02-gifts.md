## The relics that follow the calendar

### Gift Box

Right-click fills it with presents (nine). Pick a wrapping colour, then **lock it for one player** by typing their name in chat - from that moment only they can open it, and only during the **gift window: December 24 to 26**. "cancel" puts the box back in your hand. You cannot open your own box, and a locked box cannot be broken by anyone but staff. Gift boxes are not craftable: they are handed out for events such as the Secret Santa.

### Advent Calendar

The calendar has a door for **each day from December 1 to 25**, and each player can open each door exactly once. No skipping, no stacking.

### Countdown board & Countdown Clock

The plaza countdown board counts down to Christmas; its placer can change the timezone (sneak + right-click cycles the ten zones). The New Year Countdown Clock does the same for midnight on the 31st and strikes twelve when it is time. Both also work as staff-placed village furniture.

### Secret Santa

Not a prop but a staff command (`/relics secret`): named players draw lots, everyone gets a fresh box and a whisper of who to fill it for. Announced on Discord before each run - check the [news page](/news).

### Good to know

* The gift window is also when Santa walks with presents (see the Xmas Town chapter).
* Filling and wrapping take one chest's worth of luck: contents come from the server's gift table, so nobody knows what is inside - not even the placer.
