## The December collection

Seventeen props make Xmas Town, and you can own any of them for your own base. All are Relics: placeable, right-clickable, and they keep working after a restart.

| Prop | What it does |
|---|---|
| Christmas Tree | Brass gears, glass baubles and a star that never dims (twinkles) |
| String Lights | Hangs on a wall or from a ceiling; full-block strands with fat bulbs |
| Advent Wreath | One more candle burns every Sunday of Advent |
| Brass Steam Lantern | Warm light with a little puff of steam |
| Snow Lantern | A yukimi stone lantern, capped with snow |
| Giant Candy Cane | Two blocks of peppermint - right-click with a dye to repaint the stripes |
| Kadomatsu | Bamboo and pine to welcome the new year |
| Hearth | Stockings on the mantle, a fire that keeps the snow outside |
| Snowman | Top hat, brass goggles, carrot nose, gentle snowfall |
| Sleigh | Right-click to sit in it |
| Clockwork Toy Train | Round and round it goes |
| Gift Box | Fill it, lock it for one player (see [Gift Boxes & Advent](/wiki?page=relics-gifts)) |
| Advent Calendar | One door a day, December 1 to 24 |
| Music Box | Right-click plays the carol playlist; sneak + right-click for songs, shuffle and pause |
| Snow Globe | Shake it with a right-click |
| Christmas Market Stall | Ginger, greens and gold for sale |
| Cookie Plate | Take a cookie with a right-click; refill it with gingerbread |

### Good to know

* The **countdown board** on the village plaza and the **Countdown Clock** and **Temple Bell** are New Year relics - same idea, different season.
* Placed props keep their old look after a resource-pack update until the chunk reloads: break and replace is the quickest way to see a fresh version.
