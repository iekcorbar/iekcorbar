## Evergreen curiosities

Not every relic waits for a holiday. The **Curio** collection lives in your builds all year. Two favourites:

### Wish Tree (tanabata)

Craft: cherry sapling + flower pot + paper. Place it, then **right-click with a sheet of paper and type your wish** - it hangs in the branches as a coloured strip, and everyone who passes can read the canopy. There is no fee and no rule about what you wish for. The tree grows into a proper cherry tree two blocks high with bamboo beside it, and since November the wishes hang in the fringe of the crown instead of a sad little seedling.

### Brass Telescope

Craft: spyglass + two gold ingots. Place it and **right-click to look through it**. Since November it is a proper full-height brass instrument on a wide tripod with stacked tube segments - and whether the lens shows you across the map is a staff secret.

### Good to know

* Curios are purely decoration unless right-click does something - try right-click first, then sneak + right-click.
* Everything craftable shows up in `/zukan`; the rest is event, shop or quest reward.
