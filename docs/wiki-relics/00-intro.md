## Welcome to Relics

Relics are placeable props with a past: lanterns, gramophones, gift boxes, whole holiday sets. They are not blocks - you place them like furniture, right-click them to use them, and many have a mind of their own (they light, play, count down or react to the calendar).

### What you will do

* **Place a scene.** Props sit flush on the floor, wall or ceiling and keep the style of the room. The steampunk and Japanese collections fit the whole year; the seasonal sets bring a holiday to your street.
* **Use them.** If a relic does something, right-click does it. Sneak + right-click often opens a small menu (music props, countdown boards).
* **Follow the calendar.** Some relics only come alive in their season: the advent calendar opens one door a day from December 1, gift boxes only open in the gift window, the temple bell rings out the old year.

### How the pages are organised

**Xmas Town Props** covers the December collection (sleigh, tree, advent calendar, music box and all). **Gift Boxes & Advent** explains the two relics that follow the real calendar. Every relic description tells you what right-click does.
