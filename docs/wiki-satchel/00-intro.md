## Welcome to Satchel

Satchel adds backpacks: a bag you carry in your inventory that holds a lot more than the slot it takes up. Right-click it and it opens like a chest. No mods needed, it all works in the normal game.

### The six backpacks

| Backpack | Storage | Upgrade slots |
|---|---|---|
| Canvas Backpack | 18 | 1 |
| Copper Backpack | 27 | 2 |
| Iron Backpack | 36 | 3 |
| Gold Backpack | 45 | 3 |
| Diamond Backpack | 45 | 4 |
| Netherite Backpack | 45 | 5 |

* **Open it.** Hold the backpack and right-click. Sneak + right-click on a block does nothing, so you can still place things.
* **Upgrade slots.** The bottom row of the backpack has slots for upgrades. See the Upgrades page.
* **Keep your things.** Higher tiers are bought too. Move your things over by hand, upgrades included.
* **No backpacks in backpacks.** A backpack cannot be put inside another one.
* **Safe on death.** The contents live in the backpack item itself. If you die, the backpack drops with everything in it.

### Crafting

Backpacks cannot be crafted. Buy them from a wandering trader (look for them while you travel) or from the shop NPC. Upgrades are crafted: see the Upgrades and Recipes page, or look them up in Zukan.

Browse every recipe in Zukan: `/zukan @satchel`.
