💡 About This Guide

This handbook covers the website — your account, profile, and everything reachable from the navigation bar. It doesn't cover in-game mechanics, jobs, or the economy, which are best learned in-game and on the Wiki.

§ 1 — Part I: Getting Started
Welcome to Iekcorbar

Iekcorbar is a custom RPG Paper Minecraft server built around mechanical steampunk aesthetics, Japanese folklore, and cherry-blossom scenery — gears and cogs alongside sakura petals and shogunate-style architecture. Beyond the in-game world, the website is where you manage your account, track your stats, chat with other players, and keep a personal journal of your journey.

Server Address

85.202.160.151:2267

Add this as a server in your Minecraft Java Edition client. You'll need to be connected to it partway through account registration — see §2.

ℹ️ Live Server Status

The homepage shows whether the server is online right now, how many players are connected, and a live Discord member count — check it before you try to join.

§ 2 — Part I: Getting Started
Creating Your Account

Registration links your website account to a specific Minecraft username, and proves you actually own that username by asking you to read a one-time code from inside the game itself. It happens in three steps, in this exact order:

  1. Fill in the registration formClick "Sign In / Register" and switch to "No account? Join the crew now." Enter your Email Address, an Access Key (Password), and your exact Minecraft IGN (In-Game Name). Submitting this immediately whitelists that username on the server.
  2. Join the Minecraft serverOpen Minecraft and connect to 85.202.160.151:2267 using the exact username you just registered with. You must be online in-game for the next step to work.
  3. Request your codeBack on the website, click "Send Verification Code." A message is sent straight to your in-game chat with a 6-digit code — look for it in Minecraft, not your email.
  4. VerifyType that 6-digit code into the website and submit. Your account is created immediately and you're logged in.

⚠ Common Snags

If "Send Verification Code" doesn't work, it almost always means you're not actually connected to the server under the exact username you registered with — double-check both. The code also expires after a while; if it stops working, restart registration rather than trying an old code.

ℹ️ One Account, One Minecraft Username

Each email and each Minecraft username can only be registered once. If you already have an account, use §3 to log in instead — don't register again with the same details.

§ 3 — Part I: Getting Started
Logging In

Once registered, logging back in is simple: click "Sign In / Register," enter your email and password, and you're in. No in-game step is needed again after the first time.

💡 Staying Logged In

The site remembers you across visits on the same browser. If you're ever unexpectedly signed out after the site has been updated, just log back in — it happens occasionally and is nothing to worry about.

ℹ️ Forgotten Password

There is currently no self-service "forgot password" flow. If you're locked out, reach out to staff directly for help.

§ 4 — Part II: Your Profile
Your Profile Page

Your Profile is the hub for everything tied to your account — find it in the navigation bar once logged in.

Minecraft Identity
Confirm or update the IGN linked to your account.
Steampunk Wardrobe
Set a custom skin, either by URL or by uploading an image file.
In-Game Stats
Playtime, balance, Elite Coins, kills/deaths, mob kills, blocks mined, land claims, and active jobs — synced live from the server.
Registered Events
Community events you've signed up for.

Further down the page you'll also find the Anonymous Report form (§10), the Report a Bug form (§10), and your Logbook (§5).

§ 5 — Part II: Your Profile
Your Logbook (Journal)

At the bottom of your Profile is your personal Logbook — a diary that behaves like a real book, with pages you flip through rather than a plain scrolling list.

Writing an Entry

  1. Click "New Entry"Pick a date, write your entry, and optionally attach a screenshot.
  2. SaveYour new page joins the book in date order. The book opens to your most recent page by default; use the arrows either side to flip through older ones.

Who Can See It

Your Logbook is private by default. Once someone becomes your Friend (§6), they can see your whole Logbook from your profile automatically. You can also share one specific page directly with a friend using the share button on that page — they'll get a notification linking straight to it.

💡 Sharing Outside the Site

Each page also has an external share button that uses your device's normal share menu — handy for posting a page to Discord or anywhere else.

§ 6 — Part II: Your Profile
Friends

Find the Friends page in the navigation bar. It's the one place to manage requests and see who's in your crew.

  1. Send a requestType a Minecraft username and send — no need to know their email.
  2. Respond to requestsIncoming requests appear on your Friends page and in the notification bell (top of every page); accept or decline from either.
  3. Manage your crewYour current friends list is on the same page, with an option to remove anyone.
ℹ️ Why Add Friends?

Being friends is what unlocks seeing each other's Logbook (§5) and lets you jump straight to someone's profile from their name anywhere on the site.

§ 7 — Part III: Around the Site
Chat

The Chat page lets you message any other registered player directly, separate from in-game or Discord chat.

  • Pick a contact from your recent conversations or search the full member directory.
  • Send text and image attachments.
  • Your most recent conversation reopens automatically the next time you visit — no need to re-find it.

§ 8 — Part III: Around the Site
The Brewery Mini-Game

The Brewing page is a website-only companion to the in-game Brewery — recipes here match what you already know from playing, just started from your browser instead.

  1. Pick a recipeUnder the Brew tab, choose from the available recipes — you must be logged in and have your Minecraft IGN linked (§4).
  2. WaitBrewing takes somewhere between 5 and 15 real-world minutes depending on the recipe's difficulty. You can only have one brew going at a time.
  3. Collect the resultOnce ready, the quality your brew turned out (it's decided the moment you start, but only revealed when it's done) shows up on the page and joins your Collection tab.

The Blueprints tab shows barrel and distillery build guides. There's no in-game reward for completing a brew — results live entirely on the website as a personal collection.

§ 9 — Part III: Around the Site
Wiki, Events, Rules & Voting
Wiki
Lore and reference articles about the server — the best place to look up anything not covered here.
Events
Upcoming community events with dates and details; register your interest directly on the page. Community polls also appear here.
Rules
The full, current server rules. Read them before you play — staff enforce what's written here.
Vote
Links to vote for the server on external listing sites, plus whatever in-game rewards are currently configured for voting.

The Updates page (also in the nav) is where staff post the server's changelog and announcements — worth checking after any downtime or big change.

The Projects page lists community build projects you can sign up to help with, and the Map page gives a live overview of the world.

§ 10 — Part III: Around the Site
Reporting a Player or a Bug

There are two separate forms, both on your Profile page — use the right one.

Reporting a Player

Use the Anonymous Report form for rule violations — hacking, harassment, griefing, and similar. Pick a category, describe what happened, and submit. Your identity is never recorded or linked to the report in any way, even internally.

Reporting a Website Bug

Use the Report a Bug form for problems with the website itself — something broken, not working as expected, or displaying wrong. Unlike the player-report form, this one is not anonymous: your username and email are included, since staff may need to ask follow-up questions to reproduce the issue.

ℹ️ In-Game Problems

For something happening in-game right now (an active griefer, a stuck situation), the fastest route is contacting online staff directly rather than waiting on either form.

§ 11 — Part III: Around the Site
Installing the Site as an App

The website can be installed on your phone like a native app — a home-screen icon that opens full-screen, no browser bar.

  • Android / Chrome: the browser offers to install it automatically — look for an "Install" or "Add to Home Screen" prompt, or find it in the browser's menu.
  • iPhone / Safari: there's no automatic prompt. Tap the Share icon, then "Add to Home Screen."
