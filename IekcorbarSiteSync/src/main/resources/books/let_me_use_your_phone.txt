&7a tale by &fDarkmatter86

&8The knocking came twice. The second time, through the window.
---
Deep in a wood happy and content inside his caravan
Lived an isolated, cranky and self conceited old man
He appreciated his silence to a ridiculous degree
Living a life which he described as peace and harmony
---
Friends, relatives and acquaintances he had none
Claimed they were a waste of time when all said and done
All visitors he kicked off the premises with a steel toe-capped boot
Any intruders he had a two cylinder shotgun ready and loaded to shoot
---
All his food and water he picked up from natural sources
He preferred the good old flame from costly gas and electrical resources
He did not share any of his wealth he had collected over the years
Such was the life of this man of cold soul and dry tears
---
One rainy day while reading a book in the blackest of night
There was knocking at the door that gave him a fright
He put his book down to see who would be calling
Only to find to his disappointment a young girl lost and bawling
---
She asked politely to the man if she could use his phone
So she could get a ride that would take her home
For she was lost and soaking from the heavy downfall
But the man was adhesive to her pleas and pitiful call
---
He slammed the door in her face without so much as uttering a word
As if he could be taken in by the voice of a stranger he’d never even heard
She knocked again, this time more vicious and louder
It caused such a noise that the man found it difficult to ignore her
---
He got his shotgun from the cupboard and swung open the door
But instead of firing a shot he looked into the blackness with awe
Such strangeness was left embedded on his cold face
For the young lady was gone, disappeared without a trace
---
Reluctantly, he went outside into the pouring rain
He wanted to make sure she wouldn’t show up again
He checked up and down all six corners of the van
Only to return inside as a wet, shivering and confused old man
---
Rubbing a towel over his head, feeling slightly lightheaded
He got out of his wet clothes and went to bed all fears embedded
He listened to the sound of the rain trickling against the window
Lightning started to come down creating a fascinating light show
---
Then there came the same familiar knock that occurred before
Annoyed, he took his shotgun and aimed it at the door
The knocking intensified the more he waited
And eventually with a push of his finger, the door was decimated
---
The noise had ceased once more
As he stood there staring at the non-existent door
He went to look out to see if she was dead
But again all he got was a feeling of dread
---
Then the lightning revealed a message etched on the window screen
That pushed him away from the window with a very loud scream
The message was short as he sat on the floor frightened and alone
Reading it with heavy breaths let me use your phone
---
At first he thought it all a hallucination
Developed by the many long years of isolation
Then a loud scrape could be heard like a very loud snore
As it got ever closer and closer towards the non-existent door
---
He called out for the interference to cease
So that he could go to bed happily and in peace
But his pleas of sanity disappeared through the gale
As he saw a wet and pruned figure sliding up the handrail
---
The fear coursed through his body as he looked out in distress
His old heart was pumping faster and faster like a giant press
Failing to shoot, he tried to scream but he just could not speak
His endurance to the situation had now reached its peak
---
He could barely see what was there coming towards him
All he could see was a shadow with outer features so grim
In his dying breath he accepted the plagued request
His beating heart skipping many a beat in his cold chest
---
By morning, the man was lying on the floor dead
His eyes wide open with that constant feeling of dread
A little note was now resting on his lifeless chest
That read ‘thank you for letting me use your phone and have a nice rest’
