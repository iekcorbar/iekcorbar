&lThe basics&r

A source turns. Everything touching it turns too: shafts, cogwheels, machines.

&6RPM&r = speed.
&6Tq&r = load. Each machine adds load, each source adds capacity.

Too much load and the network &cjams&r: it stops and puffs steam. Add power or remove a machine.
---
&lThe basics&r

Faster than &c192 RPM&r and a part snaps its breaker pin.

Two sources fighting each other, or gears locked in a loop, also jam.

Parts are protected like blocks. Pick one up with two left-clicks.
---
&lPower: Hand Crank&r

Against a shaft end. Hold right-click, sneak to reverse.

16 RPM, 128 Tq. Makes you hungry.

One crank runs 2 millstones, 1 hammer, 2 fans or 1 pumpjack.
---
&lPower: Water Wheel&r

Needs &9flowing&r water. Each side pushed by water adds 4 RPM, to a max of 8.

256 Tq and it never stops.

Use one side only: water on both sides cancels out.
---
&lPower: Windmill&r

Sails about 10 blocks wide. Place it on a block; the shaft leaves from the back. Sneak to flip.

4 RPM low, 6 above Y 64, 8 above Y 100. Rain +2, storm +4.

64 Tq per RPM. The sails need free air in front, or it stops.
---
&lPower: Steam&r

&6Boiler&r: needs a water source next to it and fuel (coal, charcoal, blaze rod, lava bucket, fuel canister, or a hopper).

&6Engine&r: place it on top. A shaft leaves from the side.

24 RPM and 1536 Tq. Coal lasts 80 seconds.
---
&lMoving rotation&r

&6Shaft&r carries rotation straight.

&6Cogwheel&r: two small side by side turn opposite. Large next to small diagonally: the small one turns twice as fast.

&6Gearbox&r sends rotation round a corner.
---
&lMoving rotation&r

&6Clutch&r: a shaft until redstone cuts the far side off.

&6Gearshift&r: with redstone the far side reverses. Good for belts.

&6Chain Sprocket&r: link two with iron chains, up to 12 blocks, same axis, same plane. One chain per block.
---
&lGauges&r

&6Speed Gauge&r shows RPM.
&6Stress Gauge&r shows load against capacity. Needle at the end: about to jam.
---
&lMillstone&r

Takes power from &obelow&r. Right-click items in, or a hopper on top. Empty hand takes the results.

Cobblestone to gravel, gravel to sand, bone to bone meal, wool to string, wheat to flour, flowers to double dye.
---
&lSteam Hammer&r

Presses items lying under it, one per strike. Shaft through the side.

Ingots to plates, sugar cane to paper, snow block to ice, stone to cracked stone.

A hopper under it collects the results.
---
&lBellows Fan&r

Blows out of its front, 4 to 12 blocks. Put something in the stream and items behind it change after 5 s:

&6Lava&r smelts
&6Fire&r smokes
&9Water&r washes
&5Soul fire&r haunts

Washing turns concrete powder into concrete.
---
&lCrushing Wheels&r

Two wheels side by side, one powered. Throw items on top; results fall out between them.

Ores give more than mining. Mine with Silk Touch, then crush.
---
&lKettle and Mixer&r

Kettle holds water and ingredients. The mixer on top stirs, powered from above.

Heat under the kettle: fire, campfire, lava or magma.

&6Brass&r: 3 copper + 1 zinc. Also bread, cake, mud, clay, gunpowder and more.
---
&lDrill and Saw&r

Power from behind. They break the block in front.

&6Drill&r: stone in 2 s at crank speed. Never breaks chests or parts.
&6Saw&r: cuts wood and plants, and fells the whole tree.

They only break where the player who placed them is allowed.
---
&lConveyor Belt&r

Lay pieces in a row. Power a shaft at the side. Reverse the rotation and the belt reverses.

It carries items, mobs and players. At the end it feeds a chest, barrel, hopper or kettle.

Under a hammer, items wait to be pressed.
---
&lKarakuri Arm&r

A robot arm, powered from below. Fit a tool head: Gripper, Placer or Drill.

Teach up to 8 points: sneak + right-click the arm, then click blocks. Sneak + click the arm again to finish.

Programs come from a Book and Quill. Punch Cards copy them. Type &o/sw help&r for the guide.
---
&lTrains&r

Normal rails. Right-click a rail with a locomotive, then click the rail behind it with a wagon.

&6W&r steam, &6S&r brake, &6Jump&r whistle, &6Sneak&r get off.

Fuel: coal 2 min, blaze rod 3, coal block 20, fuel canister 5.
---
&lTrains&r

Passenger Wagon: 4 seats.
Cargo Wagon: 27 slots.

&6Station&r beside the track, name it in an anvil.
&6Signal&r is red when a train is ahead or when it gets redstone.

&6Autopilot&r: right-click the locomotive with a clock. It stops at stations and turns at the end of the line.
---
&lOres&r

&6Zinc&r Y -48 to 72
&6Tin&r Y -24 to 96
&6Sulfur&r deep, and in the Nether

They mine like real ores. Fortune gives more, Silk Touch gives the block. Smelt raw zinc and tin.
---
&lPlates&r

The Steam Hammer turns iron, copper, gold and brass ingots into plates. You need them for most machines.

Brass: 3 copper + 1 zinc in a heated mixer.
Sulfur + coal in the mixer: gunpowder.
---
&lOil&r

About 1 chunk in 12 holds oil, more in deserts, badlands and oceans. The &6Oil Dowsing Rod&r points the way.

&6Pumpjack&r pumps 32 mB/s. &6Pipes&r join on their own. &6Tanks&r hold 16 buckets.
---
&lOil&r

&6Refinery&r (heat below) makes 60% fuel and 40% chemical waste.

&cGive the waste its own tank.&r If it has nowhere to go, it poisons the chunk: dead grass at 300, poison at 600.

Canisters from tin carry 1000 mB.
---
&lBuilding blocks&r

32 steampunk and Japanese blocks in four styles. Each has stairs and a slab.

Craft 3 base items + 1 accent in a 2x2 square for 4 blocks.

Look them up with &o/zukan&r.
