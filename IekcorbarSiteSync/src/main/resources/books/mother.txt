A WOMAN ONCE HAD A BABY
HER LITTLE PRIDE AND JOY
A FOUR POUND BALL OF FLESH
GINGER HEAD WAS THIS BOY
---
BUT ONE FATEFUL DAY
DEATH CAUGHT UP WITH THIS KID
LEAVING THE WOMAN BROKEN
WITH A BUCKET OF TEARS WITHOUT A LID

TO REPLACE HER DECEASED SON

ONE DAY SHE GOT A CALL

ANOTHER WOMAN WAS ON THE PORCH
ASKING FOR HER LITTLE GIRL BACK
---
OUR WOMAN WAS NATURALLY CURIOUS
AND PUZZLED AT THIS SUDDEN CLAIM
SHE TOLD HER THAT THIS WAS HER KID
AND THAT THE TWO OBVIOUSLY LOOK THE SAME
---
SHE CLAIMED THAT SHE HAD STOLEN HER CHILD FROM THE LOCAL PARK
MAKING OFF WITH HER IN QUICK HASTE
AND THAT SHE HAD FOLLOWED HER ALL THE WAY HOME
BUT OUR LADY JUST SAID ‘YOUR TIME HAS JUST GONE TO WASTE’
---
BUT THE WOMAN AT THE DOOR WAS NOT APPEASED
AND KEPT UP WITH HER VICIOUS RANT
SHE EVEN THREATENED TO CALL THE POLICE IF SHE DIDN’T
BUT AGAIN, THE ANSWER WAS SIMPLY ‘I’M AFRAID I CAN’T’

SHE STORMED OFF DOWN THE ROAD IN A FURIOUS RAGE
DIALLING NUMBERS WITH THE GREATEST OF PLEASURE

THE POLICE CAME BY NEXT DAY WITH A WARRANT
AND TOOK THE LITTLE GIRL AWAY
OUR WOMAN STRUGGLED WITH DESPERATION
AS SHE NOW LIVES IN A CELL TO THIS DAY
