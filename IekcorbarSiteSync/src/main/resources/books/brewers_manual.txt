&lThe basics&r

A drink is a recipe you followed. Every bottle remembers what went in and what you did to it.

&6Three rules&r:
&81. &7Times are real minutes. A cask keeps working while you sleep.
&82. &7The closer to the recipe, the better the drink.
&83. &7The bottle itself says its next step.
---
&lThe cauldron&r

Set a cauldron, fill it with water (right-click a water bucket).

&6Heat below&r = cooking. Fire, lava, magma, a campfire or a lit furnace directly under it.
&6No heat&r = mixing.

Every recipe's first step says which one it wants.
---
&lThe cauldron&r

Right-click with each ingredient - one per click, up to 64 in the pot. The cauldron must be full of water first.

Empty hand = report: what is inside and how long it has been going.
---
&lBottling&r

Right-click with a &6glass bottle&r. One cauldron fills three bottles, and the brew's history travels with the glass.

A grey &oUnfinished&r name means steps remain - the lore says exactly which: &8"Next: distill it (2x)".
---
&lBottling&r

Done? The bottle shows its name, colour, stars and effects right in the tooltip.

A brew that matches no recipe turns into a &8Murky Brew&r. One sip makes you see things. It is how you learn.
---
&lThe Steam Still&r

Spirits like Shochu, Whiskey and Steam Whisky are distilled.

Craft one: a barrel, two iron ingots, blaze powder and a glowstone dust, together in any order.
---
&lThe Steam Still&r

Put bottles inside, add a &6glowstone dust&r, and heat the still from below like a cauldron.

Every &620 seconds&r it spends one dust and gives the bottles inside (up to three stacks) one distill run.

Right-click the still to ask what it is doing.
---
&lThe Steam Still&r

The still is a real container: &6hoppers feed bottles in and take them out&r. A belt from the cauldron into the still, and the next still in line - that is a distillery.
---
&lSake casks&r

Many drinks round off in wood. Craft: six planks around two iron nuggets, one wood per cask.

Twenty real minutes is one year, &ooffline too&r. Whiskey wants eighteen; umeshu is happy with three.
---
&lSake casks&r

Ten kinds, from honest oak to the lacquered &6copper cask&r (copper and iron). Recipes name the wood they want.

Wrong-but-familiar woods (oak for dark oak) still count, just less. Hoppers work with casks too.
---
&lQuality&r

When every step is done, the server grades how closely you followed the recipe:

&640%&r ingredients &7(right ones, right amounts)&r
&625%&r time &7(cook or mix)&r
&615%&r distill runs
&620%&r years + wood

Harder recipes want a steadier hand.
---
&lQuality&r

The grade is one of ten points - up to &6five stars&r - and picks the name (poor / normal / good band), the effects, and how harsh the morning after is.

Check any recipe again with &o/zukan&r or the recipe book.
---
&lThe garden&r

Eleven crops, all here for a drink: barley, hops, grapes, ume plums, yuzu, peaches, cherries, strawberries, blueberries, juniper and ginger.

Bushes and dwarf trees &6regrow&r when picked; barley and ginger are dug up and replanted.
---
&lThe garden&r

Seeds come from the fruit itself - one fruit makes a couple of seeds.

Wild patches grow &6ripe&r bushes in the biomes each plant likes. Go look.
---
&lDrinking&r

Good drinks give real effects: speed, strength, luck, night vision. Better quality, better effects, milder rough ones.

Alcohol is a debt: you sway, slur, and at the limit you pass out and wake with a hangover.
---
&lDrinking&r

Check yourself with &o/brew drunk&r.

Two sneaking friends with drinks in hand will cheers - &o"Kanpai!"&r is a toast of its own.

Pace yourself. The best brew is the one you remember.
---
&lFirst brew&r

&6Nigori Sake&r in ten minutes:
&81. &7Find steamed rice (Kitchen) or barley.
&82. &7Cauldron, water, 5 rice + 2 sugar.
&83. &7Fire below, cook &66 minutes&r.
&84. &7Bottle it. Stars appear in the lore.
