&l&fThe Day the Cat Came Home&r

&7a tale by &fDarkmatter86

&8The dog only ever wanted someone to play with.
---
THEY SAY A DOG IS MAN’S BEST FRIEND
AND NO TRUER WORD COULD BE SPOKEN
AS WAS THE DOG IN THIS STORY
WHOSE BOND WITH ITS MASTER COULD NEVER BE BROKEN
---
THE MASTER WORKED LONG HOURS
WHICH MEANT OUR DOG WAS ALWAYS ALONE
WAITING PATIENTLY AT THE DOOR
BUT YET ALWAYS EXCITED WHEN HE CAME HOME
---
THAT WAS UNTIL THE MASTER CAME HOME WITH A CAGE
AS OUR DOGS NOSE BEGAN TO SNIFF AND SNORT
HE INTRODUCED A LITTLE BLACK CAT TO ITS NOSE
AND WAS DISAPPOINTED FOR THAT WASN’T WHAT IT FIRST THOUGHT
---
NEVERTHELESS, THE CAT WAS NOW APART OF THE FAMILY
SPENDING MANY A DAY WITH THE DOG
BUT NOT WITH BALLS AND CHEW TOYS
INSTEAD CLEANING ITSELF AND BEING A COUCH HOG
---
THIS WAS NO FUN, THE DOG WANTED TO PLAY
SO HE TOOK HIS FAVOURITE BALL AND GAVE IT TO THE CAT
BUT INSTEAD OF THROWING IT BACK
IT PLAYED WITH IT ON ITS OWN AND THAT WAS THAT.
---
THE DOG TRIED TO GET HIS FAVOURITE BALL BACK
ONLY TO GET SCRATCHED AND HISSED AT
HE YELPED AND BARKED WITH RAGE
GROWLING AT THIS BALL THEIVING COUCH HOGGING CAT
---
THE CAT RUFFLED UP ITS FUR
THEN POUNCED ON OUR DOG’S BACK WITH ITS CLAWS
QUICKLY IT RAN OUT THE FRONT ROOM
AS THE DOG GAVE CHASE POUNDING ITS PAWS
---
BY THE TIME THE MASTER CAME BACK
THE PLACE WAS IN A DREADFUL MESS
GLASS BROKEN, BOOKS OFF THE SHELF
THE CULPRITS, TAKE A WILD GUESS
---
THE MASTER WENT UPSTAIRS TO THE BEDROOM
HE FOUND HIS DOG HIDING BENEATH THE BED
IT ALREADY KNEW IT WAS IN BIG TROUBLE
WHILE THE CAT HAD ALREADY FLED
---
THE NEXT FEW DAYS WERE THE SAME
A FEELING OF HATRED GREW BETWEEN THE PAIR
BUT ALWAYS THE DOG GOT THE WORST OF IT
WHILE THE CAT WAS ALWAYS QUICK TO GET OUT OF THERE
---
EVENTUALLY THE MASTER WARMED UP TO THE CAT
AS IT REGULARLY LAY HAPPILY ON THE MASTER’S LAP
AS FOR OUR DOG NOW TAKING A BACKSEAT
SPENT MANY DAYS A LONELY NAP
---
IT CAME TO THAT POINT THAT THE DOG HAD ENOUGH
REMINISCING ON ALL THE GOOD TIMES BEFORE
NOW EVERYTHING WAS DRASTICALLY DIFFERENT
TIME TO BOOT THE CAT PERMANENTLY OUT THE FRONT DOOR
---
AFTER THE MASTER LEFT, THE DOG WENT TO HAVE ITS BREAKFAST
ONLY TO FIND ITS BOWL EMPTY AND LICKED CLEAN
THE CAT WAS NEXT TO IT EATING ITS OWN GRUB
THE DOG WAS NOW MORE ANGRY THAN IT HAD EVER BEEN
---
FEROCIOUSLY, IT CHASED THE CAT THROUGH THE HOUSE
UP THE STAIRCASE TO THE BEDROOM FLOOR
WHERE IT EVENTUALLY CORNERED IT
AT THE VERY FAR END OF THE CORRIDOR
---
NOW I WILL NOT GO INTO THE DETAILS OF WHAT HAPPENED AFTER THAT
BUT MADNESS HAD UNFOLDED ALL OF A SUD
TEETH, CLAW AND FUR, SHRIEKING AND BARKING
UNTIL REVEALED A SET OF TEETH STAINED IN BLOOD
---
THE MASTER CAME HOME THAT EVENING
GREETED HEAVILY THE MASTER WAS KISSED
THAT’S WHEN HE NOTICED THE BLOODED DROOLING CHOPS
RUNNING AROUND THE HOUSE TO A SIGHT HE COULD NOT HAVE MISSED
---
BEFORE HE KNEW IT, OUR DOG WAS OFF TO THE KNACKERS YARD
CONSIDERED TOO DANGEROUS TO BE KEPT ALIVE
WITH ONE SHOT OF THAT PERILOUS NEEDLE
THE FAT LADY WAS SINGING IN FIVE
---
DREADFUL END TO ONE SO LOYAL
HUMAN NATURE CAN BE SO AWFUL
BUT AT LEAST THE CAT WAS HAPPY NOW IN THE COMFORT OF ITS MASTER
CATS HAVE NINE LIVES AFTER ALL
