&l&fThe Well&r

&7a tale by &fDarkmatter86

&8The voice was clear as a bell - and it was not asking for help.
---
In the distance, a voice could be heard
But strangely as clear as a bell
It caught the ears of a young lady
Who followed it all the way to an old well
---
The stones were jagged and chipped
Covered in moss and weeds
A voice inside was calling out for help
As she leaned in to hear its pleads
---
She assured its safety by shouting
Her voice piercing the dark abyss
She said to remain calm and still
And even enquired as to how it lead to this
---
The voice stayed silent for a bit
Before the story it was going to tell
I shall tell you of my fate it said
Once you get me out of this well
---
So the lady dropped a stone in the well
To check the length of the drop
But strangely she heard no splash
No knock, no smash, no plop
---
There was no signal on her phone to call the emergency services
She couldn’t even find a long piece of rope
She even yelled for assistance to help her
Could anyone hear her, nope
---
Then the voice came again
That same voice as clear as a bell
With a reluctant feeling of regret
She slowly climbed down the well
---
The walls were wet and slimy
Not even a sound of water dropped
As she slowly descended down
The voice faded and then it stopped
---
She stopped for a moment
Her arms and legs pressed against the walls
Then from below she heard a loud scream
But this one did not sound like those distressed calls
---
She jumped in fright and tried to climb out
Only to fail with every slip
The slime off the walls covered her hands
Causing her to lose her grip
---
Her shoes lost their traction
As one slipped away from the wall
Replaced with her head with a loud thud
As the darkness enveloped her inevitable fall
---
She slowly opened her eyes
Then yelled with excruciating pain
As she looked down at her legs
There revealed were many a bloody vein
---
She looked around and almost cried in terror
Bones of bodies surrounded her without so much as a whisper
She was trapped inside where she could not get out
This voice inside had tricked her
---
For days she called for help
Until the days turned to weeks
Her voice faded after a time
As her life span began to slowly decrease
---
She died not long after
Her body was now another to decay
As the voice continues to envelope the well
Trapping another soul come what may
